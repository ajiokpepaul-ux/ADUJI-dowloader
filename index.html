GETTING STARTED

Three steps to Aduji.

Download the APK, move it to your Android TV or Fire TV device, then open it to install.

STEP 01

Download the APK

Tap the Download Aduji button. Sign in to GitHub if it asks, then download the workflow artifact ZIP.

STEP 02

Get it onto your TV

Extract the ZIP to find the APK, then transfer it to your TV using a method supported by your device.

STEP 03

Install and open

Open the APK on your TV and follow the Android installation prompts. You can then launch Aduji.

Download note: This button currently points to a GitHub Actions artifact. GitHub may require you to sign in, and workflow artifacts are temporary.
